Who We Are

A Community Built
on Compassion

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit working across education, women empowerment, food distribution, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and skill development.

To unite passionate individuals and empower marginalised communities through education, skill development and sustainable grassroots initiatives.

A world where every child learns, every woman thrives, every animal is cared for, and every community lives in harmony with nature.

  • Compassion for all living beings
  • Inclusive community action
  • Environmental responsibility
  • Integrity & transparency
Section 8 80G & 12A CSR-1 NITI Aayog ISO 9001:2015

The Bigger Picture

Why Our Work Matters

Education Changes Lives

Every child who gains access to learning breaks a cycle of poverty for an entire family.

Women Empower Communities

When women are equipped with skills and confidence, entire communities grow stronger.

Environment = Our Future

Planting trees today is the most direct investment in tomorrow's clean air and water.

Animal Welfare = Compassion

A society that cares for animals cultivates the empathy needed to care for one another.

No One Should Go Hungry

Regular food distribution ensures hunger never stops someone from reaching their potential.

Skills Drive Employability

Practical training connects youth with real world opportunities and builds the economy.
